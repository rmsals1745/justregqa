## GP2. Diligence
"In conducting its business activities, a licensed or registered person should act with due skill, care and diligence, in the best interests of its clients and the integrity of the market."

Source: https://www.sfc.hk/en/Rules-and-standards/Codes-and-guidelines/Codes/Code-of-Conduct-for-Persons-Licensed

## GP5. Information for clients
"A licensed or registered person should make adequate disclosure of relevant material information in its dealings with its clients."

Source: https://www.sfc.hk/en/Rules-and-standards/Codes-and-guidelines/Codes/Code-of-Conduct-for-Persons-Licensed

## 5.1 Know your client: in general
"A licensed or registered person should take all reasonable steps to establish the true and full identity of each of its clients, and of each client's financial situation, investment experience, and investment objectives. Where an account opening procedure other than a face-to-face approach is used, it should be one that satisfactorily ensures the identity of the client."

Source: https://www.sfc.hk/en/Rules-and-standards/Codes-and-guidelines/Codes/Code-of-Conduct-for-Persons-Licensed

## 5.1A Know your client: investor characterization
"A licensed or registered person should, as part of the know your client procedures, assess the client's knowledge of derivatives and characterize the client based on his knowledge of derivatives." (para. 5.1A(a); sub-paragraph (b) sets out what to do where a client without knowledge of derivatives wishes to purchase a derivative product.)

Source: https://www.sfc.hk/en/Rules-and-standards/Codes-and-guidelines/Codes/Code-of-Conduct-for-Persons-Licensed

## 5.2 Know your client: reasonable advice
"[T]he licensed or registered person should, when making a recommendation or solicitation, ensure the suitability of the recommendation or solicitation for that client is reasonable in all the circumstances."

Source: https://www.sfc.hk/en/Rules-and-standards/Codes-and-guidelines/Codes/Code-of-Conduct-for-Persons-Licensed

## 5.5 Know your client: complex products
"[A] licensed or registered person providing services to a client in complex products should ensure that – (i) a transaction in a complex product is suitable for the client in all the circumstances; (ii) sufficient information on the key nature, features and risks of a complex product is provided ... (iii) warning statements ... are provided to the client in a clear and prominent manner." "Complex product" refers to an investment product whose terms, features and risks are not reasonably likely to be understood by a retail investor because of its complex structure.

Source: https://www.sfc.hk/en/Rules-and-standards/Codes-and-guidelines/Codes/Code-of-Conduct-for-Persons-Licensed

## 6.1 Client agreement in writing
"Licensed or registered persons should enter into a written agreement ("Client Agreement") with each client before services are provided to the client. The Client Agreement should be in Chinese or English according to the language preference of the client ..."

Source: https://www.sfc.hk/en/Rules-and-standards/Codes-and-guidelines/Codes/Code-of-Conduct-for-Persons-Licensed

## 12.1 Compliance: in general
"A licensed or registered person should comply with, and implement and maintain measures appropriate to ensuring compliance with the law, rules, regulations and codes administered or issued by the Commission, the rules of any exchange or clearing house of which it is a member or participant ..."

Source: https://www.sfc.hk/en/Rules-and-standards/Codes-and-guidelines/Codes/Code-of-Conduct-for-Persons-Licensed

## 12.3 Complaints
"A licensed or registered person should ensure that: (a) complaints from clients relating to its business are handled in a timely and appropriate manner; ... (c) where a complaint is not remedied promptly, the client is advised of any further steps which may be available to the client under the regulatory system including the right to refer a dispute to the FDRC ..."

Source: https://www.sfc.hk/en/Rules-and-standards/Codes-and-guidelines/Codes/Code-of-Conduct-for-Persons-Licensed
