## Scope: GenAI in customer-facing applications
GenAI circular (19 August 2024): "[W]ith respect to consumer protection in customer-facing applications, the HKMA expects all authorized institutions to apply and extend the 2019 BDAI Guiding Principles to the use of GenAI and continue to adopt a risk-based approach commensurate with the risks involved." Potential risks such as lack of explainability and hallucination "could cause even more significant impact on customers."

## 1. Governance and accountability
GenAI circular (19 August 2024), principle 1: "The board and senior management of authorized institutions should remain accountable for all the GenAI-driven decisions and processes ..." including adopting the "human-in-the-loop" approach in the early stage of deploying customer-facing GenAI applications. BDAI circular (5 November 2019), principle 1: "appropriate level of explainability of the BDAI models including any algorithms (i.e. no black-box excuse)."

## 2. Fairness
GenAI circular (19 August 2024), principle 2: "Authorized institutions should ensure GenAI models produce objective, consistent, ethical and fair outcomes to customers ..." including that customers are "provided with the option to opt out of using GenAI and request human intervention", or channels "to request for review of the GenAI-generated decisions". BDAI circular (5 November 2019), principle 2(b): "customer access to basic banking services are not denied unjustifiably."

## 3. Transparency and disclosure
GenAI circular (19 August 2024), principle 3: "[T]hey should disclose the use of GenAI to customers, and, among others, communicate with customers on the use and purpose of adoption of the GenAI models as well as the limitations of such models ..." BDAI circular (5 November 2019), principle 3(c)-(d): customers can request reviews of decisions and explanations of what data and factors drive them, "not required for systems used for monitoring and prevention of frauds or money laundering / terrorist financing activities."

## 4. Data privacy and protection
GenAI circular (19 August 2024), principle 4: "Authorized institutions should implement effective protection measures to safeguard customer data." Where personal data are processed they should comply with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance and have regard to PCPD guidance on GenAI. BDAI circular (5 November 2019), principle 4(b): collect and store "only the minimum amount of data for the minimum amount of time (i.e. "data minimisation")."

## Proactive use of BDAI and GenAI in enhancing consumer protection
GenAI circular (19 August 2024): "[A]uthorized institutions are encouraged to explore the use of BDAI, including GenAI, in enhancing consumer protection." Examples include "identification of customers who are vulnerable and require more protection and education."
